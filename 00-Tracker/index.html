How completion works

A topic is completed only when all five requirements are done: Theory, the Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced exercises, and the final Assessment. Opening a file never completes anything. An exercise can be marked done only after you write code under its # YOUR CODE: line (chips with a dot have code detected). The assessment unlocks once the other four are done; ask your tutor to assess you, then mark it.

Click a topic title to open its file in your editor. Progress is saved to 00-Tracker/progress.json; PROGRESS.md and COURSE-MAP.md are regenerated automatically. The page refreshes every few seconds, so new, renamed or deleted topic files show up on their own.
